# ICP / Positioning → SEO Brief

Source: *Studio Maydit — Positioning Master* (owner-supplied, 2026-07-06).
Purpose: keep every future page/post targeting the **tribe's own language**, because that
language is both unclaimed keyword space *and* what makes traffic **qualified** (not just volume).
This is the lens for the 10k-qualified-visitors goal.

## The tribe (one line)
Funded or recently-exited, **non-technical, taste-obsessed founders building AI products**
(US/UK/EU) who treat a designer as a partner, not a vendor, and are afraid of looking like everyone else.

- **NOT** "SaaS" or "tech companies" (category, not tribe). AI is the spearhead because the best,
  most showcase-able clients are AI-native.
- Sub-types: **exited operator** (retainer-best), **venture/product studio** (Tier-2 land),
  **funded first-timer** (most trigger-driven, enters on the website wedge).

## The enemy (the hook that makes them feel seen)
Looking like the **15th Claude-built clone**. AI made baseline design free → everyone ships something
that works and looks identical. Spine: **"in 2026, good enough is not good enough — taste is the moat."**

## Confirmed native phrasing → target these as search/keyword themes
Use their words verbatim in titles, H1s, meta, and body:
- "we look generic" / "looks like every other AI startup" → **why-your-ai-startup-looks-generic** ✅
- "I could vibe-code this myself… but it looks like I vibe-coded it" → **vibe-coded-website-vs-designed** ✅
- "taste is the moat" / "good enough isn't good enough" → **taste-is-the-moat-ai-startups** ✅
- "four weeks until we go live" (the canonical trigger) → **launch-ready-website-ai-startup** ✅
- "looks like a YC company" / "million-dollar design team" → **make-ai-startup-look-like-yc-company** ✅
- "look like the company everyone screenshots" / "look inevitable" (aspirational)
- "your product should look like the calibre of what you're building"
- "everyone starts with a Claude-built site, then comes to a real designer" (inside joke)

## The trigger (episodic, not daily) — content should meet these moments
Pain spikes at: **a launch / going live, just after a raise, a competitor's site going viral, a redesign,
an investor/partner meeting on the horizon.** Catch them on a trigger; don't shout into quiet months.
→ Covered: launch (launch-ready post), raise (after-raise post). **Gaps to build:** "competitor's launch
looks sharper than ours", "redesign before an investor meeting", "rebrand after pivot".

## Offer ladder (where links should funnel)
- **Tier 1 wedge:** fixed-scope launch-ready website (~3–4 wks) → `/services/ai-website-design`
- **The bridge:** polished site reveals the product underneath leaks → diagnosis, not goodbye
- **Tier 2 land:** product design retainer (money + stickiness, AI-commoditisation hedge)
  → `/services/design-retainer`, `/services/ai-product-design`
- Website is the **entry point, not the identity** — keep product depth visible so product-problem
  founders self-recognise (don't become "the website guy").

## Proof to lead with
- **Dualite** — signups <~10k → 100k+ (the outcome story; lead with it)
- **Wave, PixelFlow, AMP** — serial/exited calibre signal
- B2B/enterprise product depth underwrites the retainer tier

## AVOID (marks us as an outsider)
"synergy", "digital transformation", "leverage solutions", generic agency-speak,
"we do beautiful websites" (deliverable-led), over-polished corporate tone. The tribe is fluent and
allergic to pitch language. Write from *inside* their week, not *about* them.
